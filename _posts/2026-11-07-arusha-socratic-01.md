## Introduction to BitDevs and the Bitcoin Development Ecosystem

Welcome to the first BitDevs Arusha Socratic Seminar, held at **Bitcoin block 968,072**.

### Summary

Our first chapter brought together **44 attendees**, a mix of students and Bitcoin enthusiasts, along with **2 speakers**. Participants embraced the Socratic Seminar format, joining in open discussion rather than listening to a lecture, and many committed to attending future sessions and inviting their colleagues. Our community has since grown to **60+ members**.

### Agenda

#### 1. The genesis of Bitcoin: from the Industrial Revolution to Bitcoin

We traced how technological revolutions, from the steam engine onwards, have reshaped money, trade and society, and where Bitcoin fits in that story.

#### 2. The Bitcoin whitepaper and Satoshi's announcement

- [Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf), the whitepaper
- [Satoshi's announcement to the cryptography mailing list](https://satoshi.nakamotoinstitute.org/emails/cryptography/1/), 31 October 2008

#### 3. How the blockchain works: hashing and SHA-256

- How blocks are chained together and secured with proof of work
- SHA-256, the cryptographic hash function Bitcoin uses for mining, block hashes and transaction IDs
- Further reading: [SHA-256 on the Bitcoin Wiki](https://en.bitcoin.it/wiki/SHA-256)

#### 4. Bitcoin Core contributors and BIPs

- Who builds Bitcoin: contributors, reviewers, maintainers and node operators
- How changes are proposed through Bitcoin Improvement Proposals (BIPs)
- [Bitcoin Core on GitHub](https://github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin)
- [The BIPs repository](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips)

### Key Takeaways

- **From the steam engine to Bitcoin:** Bitcoin can be understood as part of a long line of technological revolutions.
- **The whitepaper:** the original design is short, readable and still the best starting point.
- **Core contributors:** Bitcoin is developed in the open, and anyone can review, test and contribute.
- **Participant presentations:** individual presentations from attendees made the session more inclusive and interactive.

### Next Steps

- **Contributing to Bitcoin Core:** we will follow up with participants interested in the Bitcoin Core repository and guide them on how to start contributing. A good first read is the [Bitcoin Core contributing guide](https://github.com/bitcoin/bitcoin/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Open question for next time:** why did Satoshi choose SHA-256 as Bitcoin's hash function?
