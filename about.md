BitDevs Arusha is a community for those interested in discussing and participating in the research and development of Bitcoin and related protocols. Be advised: discussion will be technical.

### Socratic Seminars

Each month focuses on one technical subject, built around real BIPs, Bitcoin Core pull requests, research papers and software releases. A moderator guides the conversation rather than giving a lecture. Topics are prepared at least a week before each meetup from sources such as Bitcoin Optech, GitHub, mailing lists and research papers.

Discussions follow the Chatham House Rule, and the discussion portion is never recorded.

### Get involved

Follow our events on [Luma](https://luma.com/user/usr-Oqo0kliWcyWDj2F), [X](https://x.com/BitDevsArusha) and [Instagram](https://instagram.com/bitdevs_arusha). To suggest a topic, [open an issue](https://github.com/Bitdevs-Arusha/Bitdevs-Arusha.github.io/issues).
